## Read-only electrical assessment

The two isolated AMC3330 paths and two ADS1115 converters support concurrent sensitive and high-range acquisition of signed probe voltage. The DC-coupled circuit permits DC and initial 50/60 Hz AC qualification. The design targets ±2 V instantaneous on the sensitive path and 600 V RMS at 50/60 Hz on the high path; ±850 V DC is an engineering ceiling requiring separate qualification. These are not tested operating or safety ratings.

Each ADC can run at nominal 860 samples/s, but the clocks are independent. Approximate ideal electrode-referred steps are 85.63 µV (sensitive) and 31.16 mV (high). AMC3330 maximum offset alone corresponds to about 3.29 mV and 0.299 V respectively before calibration. Resolution must not be represented as detection sensitivity.

The circuit measures one voltage difference, not total water current, earth-referenced potential or a complete field vector. A perpendicular field, dry/open/fouled probe, clipping or stale conversion can defeat inference from a low reading. Source impedance and approximately 1 MΩ input loading, DC polarization, motor noise, temperature, range recovery and finite bandwidth require measurement. No conductivity, GNSS or heading sensor is fitted, and validated acquisition firmware is absent.

Within the existing form factor, prioritize calibration and temperature characterization; timestamped READY/DIAG monitoring; invalid-state handling; DC/AC separation; range-overlap and overload-recovery checks; pre-run probe and ingress-sensor functional tests; motor-quiet acquisition intervals; protected harness routing; and pose/location integration after connector, power and clearance checks. Probe continuity/immersion sensing may need additional isolated circuitry and is not furnished merely by the current DIAG signals.

References: [Rev H circuit and limits](../pcb/RevH/README.md), [TI AMC3330](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/amc3330.pdf), [TI ADS1115](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads1115.pdf). Component isolation certification does not rate the complete wet instrument.

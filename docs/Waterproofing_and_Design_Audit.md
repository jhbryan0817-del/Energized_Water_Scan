# Rev-F waterproofing and design audit

Use [Rev-F physical changes and CAD checks](RevF_Changes.md) and the current [build qualification gates](Build_Qualification.md). This page replaces the earlier four-probe status summary; earlier revisions remain in Git history and the named Rev-E1/Rev-E2 change records.

The forward E1/E3 shafts, cartridges, seals, gaskets, wire entries, servo wells and outer channels are removed. The new sloping lower hull closes those former exterior routes. E2/E4 retain their two wet recesses, continuous roofs, cartridge gaskets, capped mounting pilots and potted wire entries.

The unchanged main hatch/gasket, rudder fastener caps, propulsion tube/boss and steering boot are functional sealing interfaces. The internal probe covers and electronics trays are dry service parts; they cannot independently prevent flooding after an external seal failure.

CAD checks address solid connectivity and modeled interference. They do not prove FDM watertightness or qualify the candidate radial seals, potting adhesive, gasket compression, shaft surface, dynamic tube sealing or boot durability. Verify the actual materials, tolerances and assembly under the qualification plan before water operation. No water-safety conclusion follows from a low measured voltage.

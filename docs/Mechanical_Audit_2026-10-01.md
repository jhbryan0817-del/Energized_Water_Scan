## Verification and practical limits

The final recompute, 145-solid static check and 184 sampled probe poses pass without unintended intersections or Boolean failures; the 1,787-item timeline has no warning/error features. USB/QT, separated wire corridors, battery lift and 18 selected tool corridors clear. Battery movement was checked at 41 positions with the controller installed; the E4 cover clears a 44-position inward-slide/lift sequence after harness and hatch removal. The five revised print bodies are each connected. See the [physical revision report](RevE2_Changes.md) for coordinates, fasteners, photographs and limits.

The 23 printed bodies total 1,106.542 cm³: approximately 1.405 kg if fully dense [PETG at 1.27 g/cm³](https://prusament.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/PETG_Prusament_TDS_2021_10_EN.pdf), plus about 0.283 kg of documented major hardware and additional components. The 320 × 176 × 28 mm hull bounding prism gives at most 1.577 L before flooded-channel subtraction; appendages need separate accounting. Actual slicer mass, trim, loaded displacement and freeboard remain to be established.

Four HS-65HB servos can demand about 3.84 A at the published 4.8 V stall point. Confirm the dedicated regulator's thermal/current margin and jam shutdown. The ESC's 1 A BEC does not prove steering stall margin. Keep regulator outputs separate. [Espressif](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32/esp32-devkitc/user_guide.html) requires mutually exclusive USB/5 V/3.3 V inputs; disconnect boat power before USB service.

Continue the [qualification plan](Build_Qualification.md): exact component fitting, one complete actuator, wet/dry interfaces including the internal stern-tube path, actual harness/battery handling, loaded flotation and steering in controlled unenergized water. These CAD corrections do not qualify sensing accuracy or establish that water is safe.

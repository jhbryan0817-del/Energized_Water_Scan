# Rev-F print and procurement schedule

The current live Fusion design is **Rev-F, 2 October 2026**: two retained probes, rear starboard controller, a shorter aft-shifted cassette and a narrowed lower hull.

Use the [current nineteen-piece print list, quantity changes and hardware holds](RevF_Procurement.md), [physical changes and CAD evidence](RevF_Changes.md), and [assembly guide](Wiring_and_Assembly.md).

Only print bodies **01, 02, 03, 04, 09, 11, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 23, 25, 27, 29, 30 and 31** are current. Parts 22, 24, 26 and 28 are removed. Parts 01, 03, 18, 30 and 31 have new geometry in this revision; several electronics components and their retained bridges move without being resized.

BOM.xlsx, BOM.csv and the connected Google Sheet are historical four-probe references. Apply the Rev-F procurement document before ordering; old four-probe quantities and M3×8 controller-shelf screws are superseded. Do not use stale repository STL/STEP/F3D files for the current hull or trays.

No 3D export or slicer run was made. Prepare future slicing from the saved live model, verify machine envelope/support access and trial the material, holes and seals on coupons. Fit one complete actuator and the actual controller before committing to the full hull print or duplicate actuator purchases. Do not infer waterproofness, structural life or flotation from a single-solid CAD result.
